Dorm advantages

  • No summer rent, utility accounts or furniture purchase.
  • Immediate campus access and easier participation in activities.
  • Dining is convenient during demanding weeks.
  • University maintenance, security and resident-assistant support.
  • Predictable semester billing and simpler financial-aid coordination.

Dorm trade-offs

  • Less privacy, space and control over roommates.
  • The dining plan may be poor value if not fully used.
  • Guest, appliance and break-closure rules.
  • Limited cooking and less control over diet.
  • Less experience managing an independent household.

Apartment advantages

  • A shared furnished unit below roughly $850–900 per month can cost less.
  • Private bedrooms, full kitchens and greater independence.
  • More control over food, guests, roommates and daily routines.
  • Housing remains available during breaks and summer.
  • Potentially more living and storage space.

Apartment trade-offs

  • Most leases charge for 12 months, including summer.
  • Deposits, insurance, utilities and administrative fees add up.
  • Cooking, cleaning, shopping and commuting take time.
  • Per-unit leases can expose roommates to shared liability.
  • Summer subletting is competitive and should never be assumed.
